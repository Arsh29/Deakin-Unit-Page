SIT753 - Professional Practice in Information Technology

Unit code: SIT753
Unit title: Professional Practice in Information Technology
Credit point(s): 1
Study commitment: Students are expected to participate in scheduled learning activities and complete the required assessment tasks throughout the teaching period.
Delivery mode: Campus and online learning activities.

Content

This unit introduces students to professional practices used in the information technology industry. Students develop practical skills in working collaboratively, managing projects and using industry-standard tools.

Topics include agile practices, project management, version control, DevOps workflows and professional communication. Students also consider ethical, professional and social responsibilities when working as IT professionals.
