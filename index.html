Unit Overview

This unit helps students build the practical skills IT graduates need to work effectively in industry — covering everyday tools and workflows, agile ways of working, DevOps pipelines, and version control, and how these pieces fit together inside real IT teams. Alongside the technical side, the unit places strong emphasis on professional conduct, teamwork, and ethics, aiming to strengthen students' overall employability.

A particular focus is placed on gender equity and diversity within the IT sector, preparing students to recognise and respond to these challenges as future industry leaders.
